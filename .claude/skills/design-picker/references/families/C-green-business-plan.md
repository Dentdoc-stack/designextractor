## 1. Identity

A crisp corporate pitch-deck look: a white ground (about 67% of the area), heavy charcoal sans-serif headings, and **black-and-white stock photography**. Each photo is cropped by a **45° rounded-corner diamond** ("cut-corner" mask) or a rounded rectangle, and some photos carry a green duotone overlay. One bright leaf green (#5AB14D) supplies all the colour. It appears as flat fills, as corner "chips" (a small rounded triangle tucked into a page corner), and as **very large numerals** ($500M, 40-50%, +973, $117M). Pure black is the second accent: black slabs, black icon circles, black team cards. Green and black alternate deliberately (green, black, green, green), so the page reads as a two-ink system rather than a single accent. The geometry is diagonal and blunt-cornered. There are no hairline-and-serif editorial touches; it is a confident "startup / business plan" register. The emphasis tools are colour blocks and numerals, not typography nuance.

**Legal-use rules for the green (computed):**
- Bright green on white is 2.68:1, so it cannot carry text of any size, and it fails the 3:1 non-text threshold. Use it as a **surface** (fills, masks, chips) where the meaning is carried elsewhere (labels in ink).
- **Ink on bright green = 6.35:1** (#1C1C1C on #5AB14D) passes AA. Text placed on green fills should be **ink, not white**. The board's white-on-green (B. slab, team card, KPI circles) is **2.68:1 and fails even at large sizes**; do not copy it.
- **Bright green on black = 7.04:1** (on #111111) passes AAA. Green text and numerals are legal on black slabs. This is the best way to keep the "bright green numeral" look at small sizes.
- Big numerals on white: use `--accent-mid #4C9541` (3.70:1, legal ≥ 18 pt regular / 14 pt bold). If you insist on #5AB14D numerals, treat them as decorative and repeat the figure in ink text nearby (not recommended).
- Charts: bars in #4C9541 / #111111 / #8C8C8C all pass 3:1 against white. Add direct value labels in ink.

## 4. Typography

Observations [seen]:
- One sans-serif family throughout; no serif anywhere.
- Weight contrast is the main device. The cover sets "Business Plan" in a **light** weight above "Presentation" in **bold**; page titles are bold; numerals are bold or semibold.
- Neo-grotesque shapes: double-storey "a", "t" with a slanted terminal, rounded-square "o", flat-sided "$" and "%", fairly tight default tracking, mixed-case titles (not ALL CAPS).
- Caps with wide tracking appear only for the cover subtitle line.
- Body text is small, grey, 2–3 lines per block, ragged right.

Best guess for the family: **Roboto** (Light 300 / Regular 400 / Medium 500 / Bold 700) **[uncertain]**. The cover letterforms and the "$500M" figures match Roboto closely at this resolution; Heebo, Inter or Arimo are also possible.

OFL substitute via npm (verified with `npm view`): **`@fontsource/roboto` 5.3.0, licence OFL-1.1** (Roboto moved to OFL). Alternatives: `@fontsource/roboto-flex` (OFL-1.1), `@fontsource/heebo` (OFL-1.1, Roboto-derived Latin), `@fontsource/inter` (OFL-1.1; Inter is **already installed system-wide** and is the zero-setup fallback). Note [seen in the `npm pack --dry-run` listing]: fontsource ships **.woff/.woff2 only**, no TTF. For LibreOffice/DOCX, convert with fontTools (v4.66.1 and brotli are in the venv): `TTFont('roboto-latin-700-normal.woff'); f.flavor=None; f.save('Roboto-Bold.ttf')`, install the result into `~/.fonts` and run `fc-cache`. The DOCX names the family "Roboto"; when it is absent, Word falls back to its own substitutes [inferred].

## 6. Signature components (build specs)

1. **Rounded-diamond photo mask ("cut-corner diagonal")** [seen on slides 1–4, 7, 14 and 24]
   - Shape: a square rotated 45° with corner radius ≈ 12% of its side, scaled so that 1–2 of its corners fall outside the page, which gives a straight 45° edge with one blunt rounded tip inside the page.
   - Build in PIL at 300 dpi: draw a rounded rectangle on a large canvas, rotate 45°, crop to the target box, and use it as the alpha mask.
   - Image treatment: convert the photo to greyscale (`ImageOps.grayscale`, autocontrast cutoff 1%), then optionally apply a **duotone** by mapping L to a gradient #111111 → #5AB14D (green duotone as on slides 2, 7 and 13).
   - Add a 4 mm white gap where two shapes meet (draw the second shape with a white stroke).
   - Export as PNG and place inline in a full-bleed table cell.
2. **Corner chip** [seen on 1, 5, 12, 13 and 24]: a right isosceles triangle in a page corner whose hypotenuse corner is rounded (radius ≈ 20% of the leg). Legs 22–30 mm, colour `--accent` or `--black`. Use one per page and alternate corners.
3. **Big-number objective block** [seen on 4, 5, 6 and 13]: a stack of H2 15 pt Bold, numeral Bold 54 pt (`--accent-mid` on white, `--accent` on black), label 10.5 pt Bold, 2–3 lines 9.5 pt. Space-before 0 / after 2 pt around the numeral, leading 1.0. Two blocks are split by a 0.5 pt vertical rule. Add an optional arrow glyph "↑" in the same colour (Roboto has U+2191).
4. **Numbered staggered list** [seen on 8]: numerals "01/02/03" Bold 36 pt alternating green/ink, each row indented a further 12 mm to echo the diagonal, with the heading 11 pt Bold and 2 lines 9 pt to the right of the numeral.
5. **SWOT letters** [seen on 10]: 96 pt Black letters. Fill version: render the letter as a mask in PIL (ImageFont Roboto-Black) and paste a duotone photo through it, green-toned for S and O, black-toned for W and T. Fallback: solid letters.
6. **KPI circles** [seen on 18]: 30 mm discs drawn in PIL with anti-aliasing (4× supersampling). The number is Bold 22 pt inside the disc, **ink on green, green on black**. One black disc per row highlights the exception.
7. **Milestone line** [seen on 21]: a hairline with alternating large icon nodes and small dots, year above, value below (spec in R9). Draw with matplotlib or PIL as one 174 × 45 mm PNG, with text as separate native paragraphs, or as a native 4-column table (see section 10).
8. **A./B. slabs** [seen on 20]: two equal slabs, black and green, giant letter at the bottom left, items at the top right (spec in R12). The board rounds the corners (radius about 3 mm); in DOCX accept square corners or use a PNG background.
9. **Label chips** [seen on 17]: short 9 pt Bold labels on a `--black` (white text) or `--accent` (ink text) chip with 1.5 mm horizontal padding.
10. **Vertical bar marker** [seen on 7]: a 1.5 × 12 mm black bar to the left of an item heading (as a cell left border of 4.5 pt, or a 1-column narrow shaded cell).
11. **Full-bleed highlight band** [seen on 16]: a green band about 12 mm tall crossing the full page width behind one list row (the "featured" item), with ink text.
12. **Black / green cards with photo** [seen on 15]: spec in R10.
13. **Progress bars** [seen on 14]: 2 mm `--accent-mid` fill on a `--accent-tint` track. Use only for real percentages (see section 9).
14. **Running header** [seen on all content slides]: short title, 7 pt, top right. On the cover's sibling pages it is set inside a green tab (slide 17) as white text, which fails at 2.68:1; use ink on the tab instead.

## 8. Best for / avoid for

- **Best for:** business plans, investor and funding proposals, go-to-market and growth strategies, startup/SME annual updates, sales proposals, market-entry studies, sustainability or "green" initiative summaries for corporate audiences, internal strategy decks turned into documents. Industries: agritech, cleantech, fintech/SaaS startups, consulting proposals, retail/FMCG growth plans. Tone: confident, optimistic, numbers-forward. Audience: investors, executives, board, partners. Length: **8–30 pages**, best when each page has one idea and ≤ 300 words.
- **Avoid for:** long academic or policy reports (60+ pages, dense prose, footnotes), legal or regulatory filings, healthcare or clinical documents (green connotes "go/eco", not clinical), sombre topics (risk incidents, humanitarian, obituary-like), government audits that need neutrality, anything where stock photography of business people would look unserious. It is also a poor fit for brands with a red, orange or other competing accent.

## 9. Do not copy

- **White text on bright green** (team card, B. slab, KPI circles, green tab header): 2.68:1, fails AA at every size. Use ink on green, or white on `--accent-deep`.
- **Bright green numerals on white** at 2.68:1: swap to `--accent-mid #4C9541` (3.70:1).
- **Generic stock business people** (suited trios walking, smiling teams, a man touching his glasses). They read as template filler; use real project, product or site photos, or the geometric fallback.
- **Meaningless metric circles** ("Strengths 91%", "Weaknesses 29%"): percentages with no definition. Only show KPI circles for measured quantities with units and a source.
- **Skill progress bars** for "Experience": pseudo-quantitative. Use years, credentials or a list instead.
- **Decorative icons in every block** (clip-art gears, people): they add noise in print; keep icons only in contact/milestone nodes, or replace them with numbers.
- **Typos and filler**: "Market Defination", "$05M", "For stay with us!" and lorem-style 3-line blurbs.
- **Slide density habits**: 2-line paragraphs everywhere, tiny grey body text and a running header that repeats the title on a 16:9 canvas. A report needs real body copy at 10 pt in ink.
- **The abstract 3D "X" render** on the cover: it is template stock art. Rebuild the X as flat geometry or with the client's own photo.
- Overusing the diamond: at most **one diamond mask per spread or page**, plus at most one corner chip; otherwise it becomes wallpaper.

## 10. DOCX implementability (python-docx + LibreOffice render)

Context: `reportkit.py` builds everything from tables (`make_table`, `shade`, `borders`, `pad`, exact `set_row_height`) and achieves full bleeds with zero-margin sections. It has **no floating/anchored image support** [seen in the source]. The ratings below follow from that.

| Component | Method | Notes / fragility |
|---|---|---|
| Rounded-diamond photo mask | **image** (PIL PNG, alpha + white bg) | Place inline in a full-bleed table cell (zero-margin section, exact row height). Robust. Flatten onto white (no transparency reliance). |
| Cover / back-cover X artwork | **image** | Composite the whole 210 × 165 mm band as one PNG at 300 dpi. Robust. |
| Corner chip | **image**, anchored | Needs raw `wp:anchor` XML (behindDoc, relativeFrom="page") in the header part; LibreOffice usually honours page-relative anchors, but it is **fragile** [inferred]. Safer: put chips only on zero-margin pages (cover, openers) inside the full-bleed PNG, and skip them on body pages. |
| Big-number blocks | **native** (paragraph styles) | Fully robust. The vertical divider is a cell left border. |
| Staggered numbered list | **native** (table with increasing left indent per row) | Robust. |
| SWOT photo-filled letters | **image** | Solid-colour letters are **native** (a 96 pt run); robust. |
| SWOT / 2×2 grid with cross rules | **native** (2×2 table, inside borders only) | Robust. |
| KPI circles | **image** (PNG disc with number baked in, or disc PNG + native label below) | DrawingML ellipses are a **shape** option but fragile in LO (text in shapes misaligns). Bake the number into the PNG and repeat it in alt text. |
| Milestone line + nodes | **image** for the line/nodes; text **native** in a matching 4-column table below | An all-native alternative: a table row with a bottom border and "●" glyphs centred (Roboto/Inter have U+25CF). That is robust but less exact. The vertical variant is all native (left border on a narrow column plus circle glyphs). |
| A./B. slabs | **native** (2-cell table, cell shading `--black` / `--accent`, 64 pt letter in a bottom-aligned paragraph) | Robust; square corners only. Rounded corners need a PNG background, which is not recommended. |
| Label chips | **native** (run shading `w:shd` on the run; `reportkit` already writes `shd` in rPr order) | LO renders run shading tightly with no padding; add non-breaking spaces as padding. Mostly robust. |
| Vertical bar marker | **native** (a cell left border of 4.5 pt `--black`) | Robust. |
| Full-bleed highlight band | **native** in zero-margin sections (full-width shaded row); in normal sections use a negative table indent (`tblInd` −18 mm, width 210 mm) | Negative indent is somewhat fragile in LO [inferred]; test it, or accept a band within the margins. |
| Team cards | **native** (2×3 table, shaded cells, inline headshot PNG) | Robust; square corners. Pre-crop the headshots to rounded rectangles in PIL if desired (image). |
| Bar chart | **image** (matplotlib PNG, existing `render_chart`) | Robust. Native Word charts are not supported by python-docx. |
| Progress bars | **native** (2-cell table, shaded fill cell + tint cell, exact 2 mm row height) | Robust. |
| Contents with right-aligned page numbers | **native** (tab stops, right-aligned tab at x 128) | Robust. |
| Running header / folio | **native** (header/footer paragraphs, PAGE field) | Robust. |
| Duotone / greyscale photos | **image** (PIL preprocessing) | Robust. |
| Any DrawingML custom geometry (diamonds as live shapes, rounded rectangles behind text) | **shape** | **Fragile in LibreOffice** (z-order, anchoring and text wrap shift). Avoid; render to PNG instead. |
