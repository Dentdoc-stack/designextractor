## 1. Identity

Docoro is a clinical, high-confidence corporate style built on two values: pure white and one saturated royal blue (`#053AA6`, measured). The blue is never a gradient or a tint. It arrives as hard-edged rectangles that bleed off one or two page edges and cut into the photography: a slab behind the lower third of a portrait, a full-width band that a row of headshots sits across, a white card that straddles the edge between white and blue. Headings are short, heavy geometric sans (Poppins-class, [uncertain]) in near-black on white or white on blue. Large numerals act as evidence ("4,128,020", "67,5%", "$468,52", "187x"), each with a tiny caption. The mood is clean, institutional and optimistic, close to a hospital network or health-tech annual review. The useful core for a report is narrow: **white page + one bled blue block + heavy sans + big numbers**. Around that core the template adds slide-template decoration (gradient photo washes, drop shadows, pills, device mockups, circle crops) that should not be copied.

## 4. Typography

Observations [seen]:
- Every piece of text appears to use one sans family. Headings are heavy (Bold to ExtraBold) with a geometric construction: circular "o", "C" and "O", low contrast, wide letterforms, tight but positive tracking. "DOCORO", "THANKS" and "BREAK TIME" are set in caps. Every other heading is Title Case ("Always Take Care of Your Health").
- Headings run 2–4 short lines, left-aligned (centred only on grid and team slides).
- Numerals are large and heavy, in white on blue or navy on white, often followed by a lighter unit or suffix ("187x", "$468,52/mo").
- Above blocks of text sit small bold labels such as "Sub Title Here" ([inferred] from blur), about 1.3× body size, in ink.
- Body text is tiny and light grey, regular weight. On blue, all type is white.

Best-guess family: **Poppins** (geometric, very wide, double-storey "a" visible in "Always" and "Care") [uncertain]. Could also be Montserrat; the round, wide "O" and "C" lean Poppins.

OFL substitute: **Poppins** itself is OFL-1.1, on npm as `@fontsource/poppins` (v5.3.0, checked), and already installed at `/root/.fonts/Poppins-*.ttf` (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold, plus italics). For long body text, **Inter** (`@fontsource/inter`, OFL-1.1, installed system-wide) reads better than Poppins at 9–10 pt. Poppins' width costs about 12–15 % more line length. Recommendation [inferred]: Poppins for display, headings, numerals and labels; Inter for body, tables and captions.

Relative sizes measured on the 16:9 slides (cap or x-height as a share of slide height):
- Cover title cap height: about 11 % of slide height ("DOCORO", "THANKS").
- Content heading cap height: about 5.5 %.
- Big stat numerals: about 4.5 % (stat card on slide 7).
- Body text line height: about 1.2–1.5 %.
- The resulting ratios are roughly display 9 : heading 4.5 : stat 4 : label 1.3 : body 1.

A4 portrait type scale (proposal, [inferred] from those ratios, adjusted for reading distance):

## 5. Layout recipes — A4 portrait (210 × 297 mm)

Common grid [inferred]:
- Margins: 18 mm left and right, 20 mm top, 18 mm bottom. Text width 174 mm.
- 6 columns of 26 mm with 3.6 mm gutters.
- Footer baseline at 287 mm: folio right, report title left, 7.5 pt.
- Royal blocks always bleed to at least one page edge. In DOCX, give those pages their own section with 0 mm margins (see §10).
- Block depths come from the slide ratios: one-third slab = 99 mm; full-width band = 36–48 mm; corner block = 55–60 % of the width.

**R1. Cover (photo or no photo)**
- Royal field from 0 to 190 mm, full bleed.
- Top-left kicker at (18, 24) mm, 11 pt caps white.
- Title at (18, 120) mm, 54 pt ExtraBold white, max 3 lines, measure 150 mm.
- Subtitle at title + 8 mm, 13 pt Medium `#C8D4F0`.
- With photo: the photo sits from y 150 to 245 mm, x 60 to 210 mm (bleeds right), straddling the bottom edge of the blue field. This is the band-cut move (slides 2, 4).
- Below the field on white: organisation, date and version at (18, 258) mm, 9 pt ink.
- Echo of the cover tabs (slide 1): a 92 × 6 mm royal tab at (18, 291) mm on the bottom edge.

**R2. Contents**
- White page. H1 "Contents" at (18, 30) mm, 24 pt.
- Left royal strip, x 0 to 8 mm, full height (a thin bled spine).
- Entries from y 60 mm, one per row: number "01" in Poppins Bold 14 pt deep navy (col 1), title 11 pt SemiBold ink (cols 2–5), page number right-aligned 11 pt royal (col 6). 0.5 pt `#DCE4F4` rule between rows, row pitch 13 mm.
- Optional KPI teaser band at the foot: royal, y 247 to 279 mm, full bleed, three stats.

**R3. Section opener / break**
- Royal top block, full bleed, y 0 to 150 mm (about the slide 7 proportion).
- Inside it: section number "02" at (18, 40) mm, 14 pt `#C8D4F0`; title at (18, 95) mm, 36 pt Bold white, 2–3 lines.
- Below on white: standfirst at (18, 165) mm, 13 pt Inter ink, measure 120 mm, plus a "This section covers" list in 10 pt.
- Photo variant: a 112 × 84 mm photo at x 98 mm, y 108 mm, overlapping the block's bottom edge by 42 mm.
- Break variant (slide 15): white page with a 44 pt caps ink heading at the top and royal in the lower 45 %.

**R4. Executive summary / KPI**
- H1 at y 30 mm. Two-column summary text (cols 1–3 and 4–6), 10 pt, y 50 to 150 mm.
- **KPI band**: royal, full bleed, y 165 to 213 mm (48 mm). Three or four equal cells, each with a figure (32 pt Bold white), a caption (8 pt `#C8D4F0`, 2 lines) and a 0.5 pt `#3764BB` vertical divider between cells.
- Below: "What this means", three numbered findings, 01 / 02 / 03 in 14 pt deep navy plus 10 pt text.
- White-card variant (slide 7): a white card, 174 × 30 mm, straddling the band's lower edge by 15 mm, holding three figures in deep navy with hairline dividers.

**R5. Narrative (body text)**
- White. H1 at y 30 mm, 24 pt; standfirst 13 pt `#6B6B70` under it.
- Body in 2 columns of 84 mm (3.6 mm gutter → 6 mm visually), 10/15 pt Inter.
- Side callout (cols 5–6): `#F0F4FB` box with a 3 mm royal left bar, holding a stat figure (28 pt deep) and a caption.
- No blue block on narrative pages. The block is reserved for structural pages, so narrative spreads stay quiet. The only blue is the folio tab: a 20 × 4 mm royal tab at the outer bottom edge.

**R6. Chart page**
- H1 plus a one-sentence takeaway (13 pt).
- Chart frame 174 × 95 mm at y 60 mm.
- Bars: highlighted series or categories in royal `#053AA6`, others in `#B4C2DE`. Value labels on top in 8 pt ink (Poppins SemiBold for the highlighted bar). No gridlines except a 0.5 pt ink baseline.
- Horizontal-bar variant (slide 24): 4 mm thick deep-navy bar over a `#F0F4FB` track of the full axis length.
- Right or bottom stat pair: two figures, 24 pt deep, with captions.
- Source line 7.5 pt `#6B6B70` under the chart.

**R7. Table page (from the pricing slide)**
- H1 at y 30 mm.
- Table 174 mm wide. The header row is royal fill with white Poppins SemiBold 8 pt caps, 9 mm tall.
- Body rows are 7.5 mm, 8.5 pt Inter, separated by 0.5 pt `#DCE4F4` rules. No vertical rules.
- A **highlighted column** (the recommended option or current year) gets royal fill with white text down its full height, echoing the blue first pricing card. Alternatively, use `#F0F4FB` fill with royal bold text.
- Numbers right-aligned with tabular figures. Total row in Poppins SemiBold with a 1 pt ink top rule.

**R8. Quote / testimonial**
- Royal block at x 30 to 105 mm, y 60 to 297 mm, bled to the bottom (slide 27).
- White quote card at x 18 to 150 mm, y 90 to 170 mm, with a 0.75 pt `#DCE4F4` border and no shadow, sitting over the block.
- Inside the card: oversized “ at 120 pt Poppins ExtraBold deep navy, top-left at (26, 96) mm, with its tail overlapping the first line. Quote text 16/22 pt SemiBold ink, measure 110 mm. Attribution 9 pt caps royal.
- Photo variant: a portrait at x 110 to 210 mm, y 30 to 150 mm, behind the card's top-right corner.
- No-photo variant: a large deep-navy figure (the evidence behind the quote) on white at right.

**R9. Team / contributors**
- H1 centred at y 30 mm.
- Full-bleed royal band at y 80 to 116 mm (36 mm).
- Four portraits, 36 × 44 mm rectangles (not circles), centred on the band (y 76 to 120 mm) at a 42.6 mm pitch, so blue shows between them (slide 13).
- Under each: name in 9 pt caps Poppins SemiBold, role in 8.5 pt `#6B6B70`, a 2-line bio.
- A second row repeats with its band at y 170 to 206 mm (slide 14).

**R10. Closing / back cover**
- Royal field from y 0 to 297 mm, full bleed.
- "Thank you" or "Next steps" at (18, 120) mm, 44 pt ExtraBold white.
- Contact block at y 200 mm, 10 pt white, in two columns.
- A white bar, 120 × 14 mm, bled off the right edge at y 150 mm, holding the URL in 9 pt royal (slide 30's URL bar, without the photo).

**R11. Findings / recommendations (extra)**
- H1. Numbered rows 01–05: number in 28 pt Bold royal (col 1), finding title 12 pt SemiBold (cols 2–6), 2-line body.
- 0.5 pt rule between rows.
- One finding can be promoted to a royal block row, full bleed, 40 mm tall, with white text. This echoes slide 26's 01 / 02 list.

**R12. References / appendix (extra)**
- White, 2 columns, 8.5/12 pt Inter, hanging indent 6 mm.
- Reference numbers in Poppins SemiBold royal.
- No blocks beyond the folio tab.

## 6. Signature components (build specs)

1. **Bled corner block cutting a photo** (slides 2, 4, 9, 12, 22, 29)
   - Royal rectangle, square corners, touching 1–2 page edges, 50–60 % of page width, 30–55 % of height.
   - A photo (square corners) overlaps one of its edges by 30–50 % of the photo's size. The photo sits *above* the block in z-order.
   - Text on the block: white, left-aligned, inset 12 mm.
   - Rule: one block per page, and never two adjacent sides with a gap.

2. **Full-width horizontal band** (slides 13, 14, 17, 21, 28)
   - Royal, full bleed left to right, 36–48 mm tall on A4.
   - Content (portraits, cards, stat cells) is centred on the band's centre line and taller than the band by 4–10 mm top and bottom, so the band reads as passing *behind* the row.
   - Variant: the band starts mid-page and runs to the bottom edge (slides 17, 21).

3. **Stat figure with small caption** (slides 3, 5, 7, 16, 20, 21)
   - Figure in Poppins Bold 32 pt. Optional suffix in the same size but Regular weight, or 60 % size ("187x", "+45.5%").
   - Caption 8 pt, max 2 lines, 3 mm below the figure, max 40 mm wide.
   - Colours: deep navy on white, white on royal.
   - In rows of 2–4, separated by 0.5 pt dividers (on blue: `#3764BB`; on white: `#DCE4F4`).

4. **Straddling white card** (slides 7, 11, 23, 29)
   - A white card, 0.75 pt `#DCE4F4` border, square corners (the template rounds them), no shadow.
   - Placed so 50 % of it sits on royal and 50 % on white or photo. It holds stats or contact details.

5. **Oversized quote mark** (slide 27)
   - “ glyph in Poppins ExtraBold at 120 pt (about 30 mm tall), deep navy.
   - The text column is indented 0 mm and the first quote line starts at the mark's x-height, so the mark sits inside the card's top-left corner.

6. **Highlighted column / card in a row** (slide 25, also slides 8 and 11)
   - In a row of 3–4 equal cards or table columns, exactly one is royal with white text. The rest are white with a hairline.
   - Encodes "recommended", "current" or "us".

7. **Highlighted bars** (slide 24)
   - Royal for the series that matters, `#B4C2DE` for context, deep navy for thin horizontal progress bars over a `#F0F4FB` track. Values labelled directly.

8. **Edge tabs** (slides 1, 30)
   - A short royal rectangle, about 92 × 6 mm, bled on the top and/or bottom page edge, offset 18 mm from the left. A cheap page-identity mark for the cover, the closing page and, at small size, the folio.

9. **Numbered list markers** (slides 4, 9, 26)
   - "01." in Poppins Bold 14 pt deep navy, or white on blue.
   - Replaces the template's white-circle icon bullets.

Most of Docoro's visual energy comes from photos cut by blocks. Without photos, the identity survives through bled blocks, bands, KPI figures and the highlighted column [inferred]. The look then gets flatter and closer to a "corporate data report", which is the right register for most reports.

## 8. Best for / avoid for

**Best for:**
- Healthcare, hospital and clinic annual reviews; health-tech, insurance and public-health programme reports.
- Impact reports with strong numbers; corporate service reviews; investor or board updates with KPIs.
- Any report whose argument is "here are the numbers".
- Tone: confident, clean, institutional, optimistic.
- Audience: executives, boards, funders, the general public.
- Length: 8–40 pages. Blocks lose impact when every page has one, so use them on about 30 % of pages.

**Avoid for:**
- Academic papers, legal or policy documents with long continuous text (the heavy sans and light body hurt long reading).
- Sombre topics (incident reports, mortality reviews) where saturated blue and big marketing numbers feel tone-deaf.
- Luxury, creative or editorial brands that need nuance.
- Brands whose colour clashes with royal blue.
- Very short memos (1–3 pages), where one block dominates.
- Text-heavy reports with few figures: the style relies on numbers to fill its blocks.

## 9. Do not copy

| Template cliché | Seen on | Why drop it |
|---|---|---|
| Blue gradient wash over photos | 1, 8, 11, 15, 30 | White text on the mid-wash `#7593CD` is 3.08 : 1, which fails AA. Washes also print muddy, and they are a stock-template tell. Use solid blocks instead. |
| Drop shadows on cards and pills | 8, 11, 23, 25, 27, 29 | Shadows look cheap in print, render inconsistently in LibreOffice, and contradict the flat hard-edged block language. Use a 0.75 pt hairline. |
| Pill callouts and pill buttons ("Choose plan", "Research 01") | 6, 18, 20, 23, 25, 26 | Buttons are meaningless on paper. Pills are UI chrome. |
| Circle icon badges and white-circle bullets | 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11 | Generic clip-art icons carry no information. Use numerals 01 / 02. |
| Circle-cropped portraits with social icons, star ratings | 10, 12, 14, 28 | Marketing-site furniture. Social icons and stars have no place in a report. |
| Rounded stat tiles and rounded cards | 3, 11, 29 | Rounded corners clash with the square bled blocks. Rounding in DOCX also needs DrawingML shapes (fragile). |
| Device mockups (laptop, monitor, phone in hand) | 20, 21, 22, 23 | Product-showcase gimmick, irrelevant to report content. |
| Light-grey tiny body text | most | It appears to be below 4.5 : 1 [uncertain]. Use ink `#141414` for body and `#6B6B70` for secondary. |
| Title-Case marketing headlines ("If You Don't Build Your Dream…") | all | Reports should use sentence-case, informative headings that state the finding. |
| Placeholder copy ("Your Name Here", "187x") | all | Every figure must come from real data with a source. |
| Pale `#EFF3EF` chart bars | 24 | 1.14 : 1, nearly invisible in print. Use `#B4C2DE` with direct labels. |
| Photo collage grids (8–10 photos) | 16, 17, 19 | Stock-photo padding. Reports need one meaningful image or none. |

## 10. DOCX implementability (python-docx → LibreOffice)

Key technique [inferred, consistent with the existing `reportkit.py`, which shades table cells via `w:shd`]:
- Give each bled page its own `section` with 0 mm margins.
- Build the page as a fixed-width (210 mm) table with exact row heights. Bled blocks become **shaded cells**, and inner padding comes from cell margins.
- Bands, blocks and straddles then stay native and render identically in Word and LibreOffice.
- Body pages keep normal margins.

| Component | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bled royal block / field (cover, opener, closing) | **native**: shaded table cells in a 0-margin section | Exact row heights (`hRule=exact`). Keep the table to one page (`cantSplit`). Plan the footer inside the table. |
| Full-width band | **native**: shaded row in a full-bleed table | In a normal-margin section, use a 0-margin section break or set the table indent to −18 mm with width 210 mm. Negative indent works in Word; LibreOffice usually honours it [uncertain, test]. |
| Photo straddling a block edge | **image (rendered PNG)** or native split | Option A: composite photo + block in PIL into one PNG (robust). Option B: crop the photo into top and bottom halves with PIL, put each in a table row whose side cells are white / royal (native, robust, pixel seams possible). Avoid anchored floating pictures (`wp:anchor`), whose position drifts in LibreOffice. |
| Straddling white card | **native**: 3-column table. The middle column is white in both rows with a hairline box; the side columns are white in row 1 and royal in row 2 | No shadow needed. |
| Stat figure + caption | **native** paragraphs (Poppins Bold 32 pt + Inter 8 pt) | In table cells for rows; dividers as cell borders. |
| KPI band | **native** shaded table row with cell-border dividers | — |
| Oversized quote mark | **native**: “ run at 120 pt, line spacing exact ~70 pt, so it does not push the text | Or a separate 1-cell column. Uses Poppins ExtraBold; embed or ensure the font is installed. |
| Quote card over block | **native**: nested table, or a 3-col table with shaded columns | The card border is a cell border. |
| Highlighted table column | **native** column cell shading | — |
| Charts (highlight bars, progress bars) | **image**: matplotlib PNG at 300 dpi, Poppins/Inter set in rcParams | Existing `render_chart` in `reportkit.py` can take the palette. |
| Edge tabs / folio tab | **native** (1-cell shaded table in header/footer) or **shape** | Header/footer tables at the page edge need a 0-margin header distance; a DrawingML rectangle is easier but fragile in LibreOffice. Prefer the PNG strip anchored inline in the footer [inferred]. |
| Left spine strip on contents | **native**: first column of a full-height table, 8 mm, shaded | A page border cannot do it: Word caps borders at 6 pt (about 2 mm). |
| Rounded cards, circle crops | **image** (if ever needed) | Not recommended (§9). DrawingML roundRect is fragile in LibreOffice. |
| Gradient photo wash | **image** | Not recommended. |
| Portrait row on band | **native**: a table row with royal side cells and image cells; the band rows above and below are white | Images inline in cells. |
| Initials tiles (no-photo team) | **native** shaded cells with centred 24 pt text | — |

## 11. Quick-select card

- **Name:** Docoro, royal block (Ref F)
- **Mood:** clinical, confident, institutional-optimistic; white page cut by hard royal-blue slabs
- **Accent:** royal `#053AA6` (white on it 9.71 : 1) + deep navy `#0E2965` for small blue type; ink `#141414`
- **Best for:** healthcare / health-tech / public-sector annual reviews and KPI-led impact reports, 8–40 pp
- **Needs photos?** No. It looks better with 2–4 strong photos (cover, opener, quote), and every layout has a flat fallback.
- **Signature move:** a bled royal block or full-width band that content (photo, stat card, portraits) straddles, plus big Poppins numerals with tiny captions
