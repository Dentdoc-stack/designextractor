# Reference analysis

Source: seven Pinterest-style contact sheets in `images/ref-A.jpeg` … `ref-G.jpeg`.
Each is a collage of slide-template pages (24–30 thumbnails per sheet). They are presentation templates, not reports, so only their **structure** transfers to a document; their surface decoration mostly does not.

**Method.** Each image was opened and inspected individually, then compared. Tags used below:

- **[seen]** directly visible in the image.
- **[inferred]** my interpretation of intent.
- **[uncertain]** font names, HEX values and measurements. Images are 474–736 px wide JPEG thumbnails, so HEX values are eyeballed from rendered color and fonts are guesses by letterform. Verify against originals before claiming a match.

## 1. Per-image analysis

### Ref A — Annual report (navy), the only document-like reference

**Palette [seen, approximate].** Navy ~`#1F3A8A` (covers, side panels); deep navy ~`#17286B` (cover shadows); light grey-white ~`#EDEFF3` (page ground); bright cyan-blue ~`#3FA9DB` ("2025" on the cover); white text.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Heavy geometric sans in caps for cover title "RAPPORT ANNUEL" (looks Montserrat/Poppins-class, **uncertain**). Section titles are small bold uppercase navy, 2 lines max. Body is a tiny regular sans. Sub-headlines on the cover are light weight.

**Structure [seen].**
- Spreads are split into a **solid navy column (~1/3)** and a pale content area (~2/3). The navy column carries title + intro text; the pale area carries the evidence (chart, map, photo). Pages 1, 4, 10 (Mot de la direction, Aperçu financier, Perspectives) all use this.
- Contents page ("Sommaire") is a flat list: label left, page number right, small type, in the navy panel.
- Org chart (Structure organisationnelle) uses a boxed hierarchy with thin connector lines.
- KPI rows ("Faits marquants"): four figures in a row (+18%, +250, 98%, +120) each with a circled icon above and a caption beneath.
- Bar chart (Aperçu financier): single-hue bars rising left to right with value labels at the top; small KPI list beside it (642 M€, 78 M€, 12,1 %, 95 M€).
- Donut chart (Répartition des revenus) in three navy tints with percentage legend (56 / 24 / 12 / 8 %).
- Dotted world map plus a vertical bar-rule beside three large stats (32, 85+, 12 000+).
- Numbered steps 01–04 in rounded white tiles over a mountain photo.
- Closing page: navy photo-free field with a one-line thanks.

**Distinctive [seen].** A back cover with a QR code and a promise sentence; stark navy-on-pale rhythm; consistent two-column split; very consistent type scale across spreads.

**Weak [inferred].** Circled icons, rounded tiles, faint geometric overlays on the covers, stock imagery (mountain, handshake-style portrait, plant in hands) are generic.

### Ref B — "Breakslide" (dark green)

**Palette [seen].** Deep green ~`#0A4D43` (base); mid green ~`#1E8C6E`; bright green ~`#23B07F` (slab highlights); near-black green ~`#062B27` (background); white text.

**Structure [seen].**
- Pages are built from **tall vertical slabs/columns** of slightly different greens, sometimes overlapping an image. Slabs are offset so they step across the page.
- Alternation of fully dark pages with a half-white page (the "Our Team", "Our Services" rows have the lower half white).
- A small row of three dots sits in the top-left of every page as a page marker (navigation device).
- Photos are cropped with a **clipped corner** (cut-off top-right) in portrait ratio; they overlap slabs.
- Icons in circles for service lists (recycle, plant, bubble, bulb).
- Small caps labels ("SERVICE 001", "MARKETING") above tiny body text.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Rounded geometric sans (Montserrat-like) in white; roles are title (medium, ~24 px equiv.), micro-caps label, tiny body. The "BREAKSLIDE" cover wordmark is heavy, widely tracked caps.

**Distinctive [seen].** Overlap of colored slab and photograph; stepped columns; dark-to-light alternation.

**Weak [inferred].** Gradient fills on slabs, three-dot motif, icon circles.

### Ref C — Business plan (green + black on white)

**Palette [seen].** Green ~`#4CB848` (accent); black ~`#111111`; mid-grey ~`#8C8C8C` for secondary text; white. Photos desaturated to mono, then green-tinted in places.

**Structure [seen].**
- Strict white pages with **one accent color and black**, plus mono photography. This reads more "report" than the others.
- Oversized numerals as the focal point: **$500M**, **40–50%**, **+973**, **109%**, **300%**, **$15M / $41M / $75M / $117M**. Label small above or below.
- Numbered lists 01 / 02 / 03 in large green numerals, the middle one in black (alternation).
- Large lettered blocks: SWOT built from huge S W O T letters with captions; A. / B. block pair.
- Grouped bar chart (4 years × 3 series: green, black, grey) with a legend on the left.
- Horizontal milestone timeline (2025 – 2028) with dots and values beneath.
- Contents list: small bullets, label left, page number right, plus a photo on the right.
- A **vertical hairline** between two stats (Financial | Market, Competitor | Audience).
- Contact page: three columns separated by hairlines (Phone / Email / Address).
- Running header: small "Business Plan" at the top-right of each page.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Bold grotesque (looks Poppins/Montserrat) for headings, black. Green used for the key word of the cover title ("Business Plan" green, "Presentation" black).

**Distinctive [seen].** Chunky numerals; color used as emphasis on a single word; green-on-black pairing; the page-corner cut (green leaf/rounded-diagonal corner) is the template's signature.

**Weak [inferred].** Rounded-corner photo crops, icon circles, corporate stock people.

### Ref D — "Prism" (teal)

**Palette [seen].** Teal ~`#3E9C85`; dark charcoal ~`#2F3234` for contrast tiles; white; light grey.

**Structure [seen].**
- Heavily rounded **pill/blob** shapes: capsule image crops, rounded cards, circles.
- Timeline ("Our Timeline Long Journey"): vertical dated list on the left, horizontal dated row below.
- Chart page: bar chart in white and charcoal on a teal field.
- Pricing page: four tiles ($0 / $30 / $60 / $90), two teal, two charcoal.
- Map page with percent callouts and a small bar chart.
- Puzzle infographic (four-piece circle) with four captions at the corners.
- A small dot after each title (a terminal punctuation mark in teal).

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Bold condensed-ish sans (IBM Plex Sans/Barlow-like), caps headings, tiny body.

**Distinctive [seen].** The dot after the title; teal block + charcoal block pairing; full-bleed chart panel in a color field.

**Weak [inferred].** Rounded everything, icons-in-squares, stock office photos. This is the clearest example of the habits to avoid.

### Ref E — "Clinicare" (aqua)

**Palette [seen].** Aqua ~`#3EC1CB`; pale aqua tint ~`#E3F5F6`; navy-ish ink ~`#1E3A5F` for the wordmark; white.

**Structure [seen].**
- Pages split **half photo / half tinted text**, or a thin tinted band across the top with white below.
- Circular photo crops overlap a pale tinted block.
- Lists in a 2×2 or 3-up grid on a tint.
- Photo-heavy; text sits in small blocks.
- Four-up team and board strips with circled portraits.
- Timeline row (dated milestones) on a tint.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Light-weight rounded sans for body; wordmark in a heavy rounded sans.

**Distinctive [seen].** Tint-on-white layering instead of strong color; lots of whitespace; image : text ratio changes page to page.

**Weak [inferred].** Circular crops, soft pastel, medical stock imagery.

### Ref F — "Docoro" (royal blue)

**Palette [seen].** Royal blue ~`#1F3CB4`; white; slate-grey frame ~`#7B8798` around every page; near-black headings ~`#111`.

**Structure [seen].**
- **A full-width blue band** cuts across the page, usually the lower third, and pictures overlap it.
- Three-up stat strips inside the band (4,128,020 / 3,837,640 / 186,861) with tiny caps labels.
- Pull-quote page: large blue quotation mark, quote text, attribution under.
- Pricing: three tiles with large price figures.
- Staff grid: portrait + name + role repeated in a row aligned on the band.
- Numbered side list (01 / 02) next to a portrait.
- Small horizontal bar chart; vertical bar chart with tinted bars and the final bar in blue.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Heavy geometric sans (Poppins-like) for headings, black on white or white on blue.

**Distinctive [seen].** Band that crosses the page with content straddling it; accent bar highlights the **last (current) bar** in a chart; a bold, short quote.

**Weak [inferred].** Gradient overlays on photos, circled icons, capsule buttons.

### Ref G — "Market" (teal, refined)

**Palette [seen].** Teal ~`#0E9E92`; off-white ~`#F3F3F3`; black text; mid-grey hairlines.

**Structure [seen].**
- **Inset hairline frame** about 8% from the page edge on every page, inside the page; content respects the frame; photos and color blocks break through it. This is the most editorial device of all seven.
- Rotated vertical title ("TRADING", "BUSINESS PRESENTATION") on a narrow tall color strip.
- Two-column key/value grids (MARKETING | PURPOSE, SERVICES | AGENTS) with a teal caps label over small body text.
- Photo and tinted bar overlap: a teal band runs across the bottom of a photo.
- Generous empty space; titles in bold black caps, very small.
- Three-column text with a teal first column; a vertical rule between columns.
- Pull-quote in a tall teal tab hanging from the top edge.
- Four-up person strip joined by a hairline.

**Typography [seen; uncertain].** Bold geometric sans caps for titles at modest size; very small body; teal micro-caps for labels. Letter-spacing on caps is wide.

**Distinctive [seen].** The frame; the hanging tab; rotated type; restraint.

**Weak [inferred].** Capsule shapes, circular portraits.

## 4. What each reference contributes uniquely

- **A**: the document discipline (consistent split, contents, KPIs, chart, donut, map, org chart).
- **B**: slab geometry and dark/light alternation.
- **C**: numerals, black + one accent, hairline-separated stats, SWOT as large letters.
- **D**: charcoal tile paired with accent tile; dot punctuation (rejected); chart on color field.
- **E**: tint layering as a quieter alternative to strong color.
- **F**: the cross-page band; highlighting the last bar; pull-quote page.
- **G**: the frame; rotated label; hanging tab; restraint and whitespace.

## 5. Open questions / uncertainties

- Exact font identities for all seven are **[uncertain]**; the skill substitutes IBM Plex Serif / Plex Sans (free, OFL) rather than trying to match them.
- Real margins and type sizes cannot be measured from thumbnails; the spec sets its own.
- None of the references demonstrate long-form body text, footnotes, dense tables or multi-page tables. Those rules are original to the spec.
