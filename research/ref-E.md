# Ref E — "Clinicare" (aqua medical clinic)

Source: `.claude/skills/report-design/references/images/ref-E.jpeg`, 474×846 px JPEG collage of a 16:9 slide template.
24 thumbnails: one large cover (≈294×175 px) plus 23 small slides of ≈143×82 px each. I cropped every slide, upscaled it 6× (cover 3×) with PIL LANCZOS and viewed each one. At this size, headline words are partly readable and body text is not readable at all. Every claim below is tagged **[seen]** (visible in the crops), **[inferred]** (reasoned from what is visible) or **[uncertain]** (a guess, possibly a JPEG/upscaling artefact). Colours were measured with PIL from flat patches of 5–30 px. JPEG compression shifts values by about ±6 per channel.

---

## 1. Identity

A light, airy clinical look. The ground is mostly white with very pale cyan tint panels. One bright aqua (≈#4DCCD4) is used only as a fill: blocks, bands, badges and highlight cards. It is never used as a page-wide wash except on the single "break" slide [seen]. Photography carries the mood: clean white labs, surgeons in blue scrubs, smiling patients. Photos are cropped into large circles with a thick white ring, or set as rectangles with a solid aqua block offset behind one corner [seen]. Headings are a dark navy-charcoal humanist sans in title case, each with a tiny tracked kicker above it; body text is light grey [seen]. The page rhythm alternates three things: a white page with one coloured panel, a tint band that spans the page behind cards, and a photo with an overlapping box [seen]. Everything is square-cornered. There are no rounded rectangles on this board [seen]. That makes it much easier to build in DOCX than refs D/G. The feel is calm, hygienic, reassuring and a little corporate-stock. Use it for patient-facing or public-sector health reporting. For heavy analytical work it needs adapting (see §8).

### Aqua usage rules
1. Aqua `#4DCCD4` is **never text** on white or tint, at any size, and never the only carrier of meaningful icons, chart marks or check marks (WCAG 1.4.11 needs 3:1).
2. Aqua is for **fills**: panels, bands, offset blocks, badges, highlight cards, the circle-photo field, decorative dots. Any text on an aqua fill is **navy #0C132E or ink #1E2733** (9.5 / 7.8:1), never white.
3. Where the board puts white text on a coloured panel (stat panel, title box, highlight card), switch the fill to **aqua-deep #0E757C** (white 5.45:1) or **aqua-ink #0B6065** for text under 10pt (7.3:1).
4. Accent-coloured *text* (kickers, numerals, links, timeline years, check icons) uses **aqua-deep #0E757C**.
5. Budget: aqua fill ≤12% of a page, deep ≤5%, tint ≤35%. Never use more than one aqua full-bleed page per section.

## 4. Typography

Observations [seen]:
- Wordmark: heavy humanist sans, double-storey "a", open "C" with angled terminals, round tittles; tight but not condensed.
- Headlines: semibold/bold, **Title Case**, 2–3 lines, left-aligned (centred on the team slides S11–S13), dark navy-charcoal.
- Kicker: a tiny line above every headline. On S10 it is clearly tracked caps; elsewhere it reads as a small, light/aqua tagline [uncertain colour].
- Body: light grey regular text, small, 4–7 lines per block, left-aligned.
- Break title: bold, about 2× the slide headline size.
- Subtitle on the cover: italic, grey, small.

Approximate relative sizes, measured on the 143 px thumbnails (1 px ≈ 6.7pt of a 13.33 in slide) [uncertain ±25%]: headline line pitch ≈ 3.6 px (≈20pt type), body pitch ≈ 2.7 px (≈13pt), so **headline:body ≈ 1.5–1.6**. Kicker ≈ 0.7× body. Break title ≈ 2× headline. Cover wordmark ≈ 3.5–4× headline.

Best guess for the family: **Open Sans** or **Nunito Sans** Bold/Regular [uncertain]. The double-storey "a" and humanist terminals rule out Poppins/Montserrat, which are geometric (both are installed but are a mismatch).

OFL substitute available via npm (verified in this session):
- **`@fontsource/nunito-sans`** 5.3.0, OFL-1.1 (recommended: soft, friendly, clinical). Weights 400/600/700/800 ship as `.woff`/`.woff2` [seen in the tarball].
- Alternative: `@fontsource/open-sans` 5.3.0, OFL-1.1.
- DOCX/LibreOffice needs TTF/OTF. Convert with the venv's fontTools: `TTFont('nunito-sans-latin-700-normal.woff'); f.flavor=None; f.save('NunitoSans-Bold.ttf')`. I tested this: the output has family "Nunito Sans", style "Bold", usWeightClass 700, so Word/LO can link the weights as one family. Install to `~/.fonts` and run `fc-cache` before rendering. Fallback if missing: Inter (installed).

## 6. Signature components (build specs)

**A. Tint panel** [seen S10, S11, S13, S16, S20, S21]
Rectangle fill #E0FEFF, square corners, no border. Two forms: (i) a full-bleed band across the page width behind a row of cards, so content straddles its edge by 15–25 mm; (ii) an inset panel with 6–8 mm padding for callouts. Text on it is ink/navy; accent text is aqua-deep. Never put a tint panel directly against another tint panel.

**B. Circle photo crop with white ring** [seen S01, S06, S08, S11–S15]
A square photo masked to a circle. The ring is 3–4 mm white (≈2% of the diameter on portraits, ≈4% on hero circles). Sizes: hero 100–200 mm, portrait 24–38 mm, avatar 12 mm. The hero circle always sits across a boundary (white|aqua, or the page edge). Keep faces in the central 60%.

**C. Stat badge** [seen S06]
An aqua disc Ø30–36 mm overlapping a hero circle's lower-right edge by about 25% of its own diameter. Content: numeral 20pt 800 navy + label 7pt caps navy. (The board uses white; changed for contrast.) One per page at most.

**D. Stat panel** [seen S03]
A rectangle in aqua-deep #0E757C, or aqua-ink #0B6065 if the labels are under 9pt. Padding 8 mm. 3–4 equal columns, each a 30pt white 800 numeral, a 8.5pt white label and a 0.5pt white (40%) divider. Optionally an outline icon 10 mm at the left. Height 45–55 mm.

**E. Offset accent block behind a photo** [seen S09, S19, S23]
A solid aqua rectangle 45–60% of the photo's width and height, shifted 8–12 mm out from one corner (lower-left by default), usually bleeding to the page edge. The photo sits on top. No-photo version: the same block behind a tint panel.

**F. Overlapping title box + square notch** [seen S18, S19, S24]
A box 90–100 × 35–40 mm overlapping the bottom edge of a photo band by about 50%. Aqua fill with navy title, or aqua-deep fill with white. A 9 mm square (white on aqua, or aqua on a photo) overlaps one top corner by half its size. Use it once per department page.

**G. Service grid with diagonal highlights** [seen S16, S22]
A 3×3 (or 2×3 checkerboard) grid of equal cells, 6 mm gutters. The highlighted cells (the diagonal, or alternating) are aqua with navy text; the rest are white with a 0.75pt #CDE7EA border. Each cell holds an icon, an H3 and 2 lines. The grid spans two background zones (tint above, aqua below).

**H. Department chips** [seen S17]
White rectangles 50 × 10 mm in a 2×3 grid on an aqua or tint field, 4 mm gaps, text 9pt bold ink centred, 0.5pt #CDE7EA border when on tint. Good for "areas covered" and "services in scope" lists.

**I. Timeline** [seen S10]
A vertical list of 4–6 rows, 20 mm pitch. Left: year 11pt bold aqua-deep. Centre: aqua disc Ø9 mm with a 4 mm white or navy icon (or no icon). A 0.75pt #CDE7EA vertical connector runs through the disc centres. Right: H3 + 1-line body.

**J. Person card** [seen S11–S14]
A circle portrait (B), name 11.5pt bold, role 8.5pt ink-soft, and optional 2 label/value rows (K). The highlight variant is aqua-filled with navy text and 6 mm padding. Cards can straddle a tint band edge.

**K. Label/value hairline rows** [seen S11, S14]
Rows 7 mm tall: label 8.5pt ink-soft left, value 8.5pt bold ink right-aligned, 0.5pt #CDE7EA bottom rule. This is the board's only table-like element and the model for R7.

**L. Checklist** [seen S19, S23, S24]
Items at 7 mm pitch. A check icon 3.5 mm in **aqua-deep** (the board's aqua checks are 1.93:1), text 9.5pt ink, 1 or 2 columns. Optionally in a white card overlapping a photo by 15 mm.

**M. Kicker + heading stack** [seen on every content slide]
Kicker 7.5pt caps aqua-deep tracked +12%, 3 mm gap, heading, 4 mm gap, body. Left-aligned on content pages, centred on people/overview pages.

**N. Dots and faint ring** [seen S01, S15]
5–7 aqua discs Ø1.6–2.6 mm in a loose cluster around a title (not on a grid), plus an optional 0.75pt ring Ø120–160 mm in tint or 40% white, bleeding off a corner. Cover, openers and closing only.

**O. Two-tone wordmark / title** [seen S01]
The first part of the name or title in navy, the last word or syllable in the accent. In a report, the accent part is **aqua-deep**, not aqua (aqua text fails contrast).

## 8. Best for / avoid for

**Best for**
- Report types: patient- or public-facing annual reviews, service/department overviews, quality accounts, patient-experience reports, programme prospectuses, health-service strategies, community health needs summaries, grant reports from health charities, onboarding/welcome packs for clinical staff.
- Industries: healthcare (clinics, hospitals, primary care, allied health, dental, diagnostics/labs), wellbeing and mental-health services, public health, public sector/local government health and social care, pharmacy, health-tech marketing, water/hygiene/clean-tech.
- Tone: calm, reassuring, clean, approachable, optimistic.
- Audience: patients, the public, commissioners, boards wanting a digestible overview, recruits.
- Length: 8–30 pages. It works best when pages are modular (one service per page).

**Avoid for**
- Dense analytical or technical work (clinical trial reports, statistical analyses, audit findings with 40+ tables): the airy panels waste space, and the pale aqua gives weak data emphasis.
- Bad news or serious-incident reports, legal/regulatory submissions: the cheerful stock-photo mood misleads.
- Finance, law, defence, heavy industry: it reads as "clinic" instantly.
- Very long documents (60+ pages): the tint/aqua rhythm gets repetitive.
- Projector or low-quality print use: aqua and #E0FEFF tint wash out.
- Brands with a warm or dark palette.
- Pick ref F (Docoro) when the healthcare client needs more authority. Pick ref D/G (teal) for a more corporate tone.

## 9. Do not copy

- **White text on aqua** (S03, S05, S12, S15–S18, S20): 1.93:1 fails WCAG. Use navy text on aqua, or switch the fill to aqua-deep.
- **Aqua text/icons on white** (timeline years S10, checks S19/23/24, "care" in the wordmark): 1.93:1. Use aqua-deep.
- **Pale grey body text**: it reads ≈#929392 or lighter; too faint for print and AA. Use ink #1E2733.
- **Title Case Marketing Headlines** ("Providing the Best Health Care to Local & International Patients"): slide-template filler. Use sentence-case headings that state the finding.
- **Generic stock clinical photos** (masked surgeons, smiling-doctor cut-outs, anatomical heart): a cliché that undermines credibility for a real organisation. Use real photos or the no-photo fallbacks.
- **Cut-out people breaking out of bands** (S07, S14): needs background removal and looks dated in print.
- **Panel gradients** (S05, S17 photo-to-aqua fade): they band in PDF/print and LibreOffice renders DOCX gradient fills inconsistently. Use flat fills.
- **Sketch line illustrations** (S21): style clash with the photography, and hard to source consistently.
- **Slide UI devices**: the nav sidebar (S20) and the literal "Break Slide" page are meaningless in a report.
- **Diagonal photo cuts** (S03/S04 [uncertain]): hard to build, and they add noise.
- **Overusing dots/rings**: keep them to the cover, openers and closing, or they become confetti.
- **Mosaic of 5+ photos** (S04): too busy at A4 and needs many good images.

## 10. DOCX implementability (python-docx + LibreOffice render)

Legend: **native** = paragraphs, tables, cell shading, borders (reportkit `shade`, `borders`, `pad`, `make_table` already cover these). **image** = pre-rendered PNG (PIL/matplotlib), inline or anchored. **shape** = DrawingML/VML shapes (fragile in LibreOffice; avoid or verify with `render.sh`).

| Component | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Tint panel, inset | native | 1-cell table, `shade('E0FEFF')`, `pad` 6–8 mm |
| A. Tint band, full bleed | native | A table in a zero-side-margin section (`section(margins=...)`) with internal cell padding to restore the 18 mm text margin, or an anchored full-width PNG strip behind text. The table route is the most robust [inferred] |
| B. Circle crop + ring | image | PIL: square crop → circular alpha mask → white ring drawn → transparent PNG at 300 dpi. Inline is safe; an anchored, bleeding hero circle is "image (anchored)", so verify its position in LO |
| R1/R3 hero circle bleeding off the page | image | Render the whole cover/opener background (aqua block + circle + dots + ring) as one 210×297 mm PNG, anchored behind text at the page origin (or in a first-page header). Put the text in normal paragraphs/tables on top. Most robust route for compositions |
| C. Stat badge overlapping a circle | image | Bake the badge into the circle PNG; don't overlap separate objects |
| D. Stat panel | native | Table row with aqua-deep cell shading, white text, cell borders for dividers |
| E. Offset block behind photo | image (or native approximation) | Bake photo + offset block into one PNG. Native approximation: 2×2 table with the aqua cell lower-left and the photo spanning the cells, which is fragile. Prefer the image route |
| F. Overlapping title box | image or native | Native: a table directly under a full-width photo with a negative paragraph spacing trick is unreliable; use a PNG band with the box baked in, or put the box *under* the photo without overlap (native, robust) |
| G. Service grid | native | 3×3 table, fixed widths, cell shading on the diagonal, 0.75pt borders; gutters via white borders (6 mm) or spacer columns |
| H. Department chips | native | Table cells, white shading on an aqua/tint parent cell (nested table) or per-cell borders |
| I. Timeline | native + image | Table: year | disc PNG (small inline image, 9 mm) | text. Connector line via left border of a narrow column [inferred]; or render the whole timeline as a PNG |
| J. Person card | native + image | Table cell(s) + inline circle PNG; highlight card = shaded cell |
| K. Label/value rows | native | Table with bottom borders only |
| L. Checklist | native | Paragraphs with a check glyph (✓ U+2713 in the font, aqua-deep) or a tiny PNG bullet; a hanging indent of 6 mm |
| M. Kicker + heading | native | Paragraph styles, `_track` for letter spacing |
| N. Dots + ring | image | Bake into the cover/opener background PNG. Never as separate shapes |
| O. Two-tone title | native | Two runs, different colours |
| R6 Chart | image | matplotlib PNG (reportkit `render_chart`) with the palette in §3 |
| R7 Table | native | Repeat header (`row_flags(header=True)`), cant_split |
| Gradient fills | avoid | Flat fills only; if a gradient is essential, bake it into a PNG |
| Rounded corners | n/a | None on this board, so no issue |

Overall: **high implementability**. Everything except circles, dots and overlaps maps to tables with shading. Circles and overlaps become pre-rendered PNGs. No DrawingML shapes are required.
