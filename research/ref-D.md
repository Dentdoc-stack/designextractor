## 1. Identity

Prism is a bright, friendly corporate template. White pages carry soft, rounded teal masses: capsule-shaped (stadium) photo crops, rounded-rectangle "slabs" that hold text, half-pills that bleed off the page edge, and circles [seen]. A single mid-teal (#379A86) covers about 16% of the board and is the only chromatic colour. Charcoal (#3C3D40) is the secondary dark for tiles and bars. A neutral grey (#C2C2C2) is used only for infographic fills [seen]. Headings are bold grotesque caps in dark grey, two lines and tightly leaded. Each heading carries a small teal dot at its top-right, after the first line. This dot is the one repeated detail that ties every page together [seen]. Rhythm comes from "break slides": a teal panel (rounded square, pill or tab) laid over a photo or white page, with a white circle number chip sitting on its edge [seen]. The mood is approachable, modern-startup and agency-like. It is about shape rather than editorial rule. It is the opposite of the existing "Slab & Rule" skill. It is generic, and it reads as premium only if the rounding is disciplined and the stock-photo habit is dropped.

## 4. Typography

**Observations [seen]**
- Headings are bold, slightly narrow grotesque caps. On the cover "PRISM" the **capital I has horizontal slab bars** top and bottom. The R has a straight diagonal leg and the M has vertical sides. This is a distinctive feature of IBM Plex Sans.
- I rendered "PRISM" and "EVERYTHING / ALL ABOUT US" in IBM Plex Sans Bold and compared them with the crops. The letterforms match closely (serifed I, R, S, G, Y).
- Measured width/cap-height for "EVERYTHING": ref 8.45 vs Plex Bold 9.04, so the ref is about 6% narrower. That suggests slight negative tracking, or a near relative [inferred].
- Heading cap height ≈ 5.4% of slide height (6.6 px of 122). Two-line headings have line pitch = 1.64× cap height, i.e. leading ≈ 1.15× font size [seen, measured].
- Subheads ("Who We Are ?", "Table of Content", names) are Title Case, semibold, in a muted teal, about 1/3 of heading size [seen; colour blurred].
- Body is very small regular grey, about 1/4–1/5 of heading size, 2–3 lines [seen].
- The template puts a space before "?" ("WHAT SERVICES WE PROVIDE ?") [seen]. Do not copy this in English.

**Best guess family:** IBM Plex Sans (Bold for headings, SemiBold for subheads, Regular for body) [uncertain; strong visual match]. The licence is OFL. npm: `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans` (v5.3.0 available). It is already installed system-wide (`/root/.fonts/IBMPlexSans-*.ttf`: Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold). Fallback for the same feel: `@fontsource/barlow` (OFL, v5.3.0) Bold [uncertain].

Relative scale ≈ H1 1 : H3 0.43 : body 0.34. That is slightly larger body than the board (≈0.25), for print reading [inferred].

**The trailing dot motif [seen]**
- It is a filled circle in accent bright, diameter ≈ 0.62× cap height.
- Its centre sits at about the cap-top line, raised like a superscript. The gap from the last letter is ≈ 0.55× cap height.
- It sits after the first line of the heading (top-right corner of the title block), not after the last word. On a one-line title it is simply at the end.
- On teal grounds it is white. On the cover it is set at mid cap height instead.

**DOCX recipe (tested in LibreOffice) [seen in test render]**
- Plex Sans has no U+25CF. Plex's own bullet U+2022 renders as a **rounded square**, not a circle, so do not set the dot in Plex.
- Use a separate run: thin space U+2009 + "•" (U+2022) in **Inter** at **1.4× heading size**, raised `w:position` = **0.28× heading size** (28 pt heading → 39 pt bullet, raise 7.8 pt = `w:val="16"` half-points). Colour #379A86.
- Alternative: "●" U+25CF in Inter at 0.6× size, raised ≈0.33–0.45×.
- Set the heading paragraph to **exact** line spacing (28 pt → 31 pt). Otherwise the raised run inflates the line.
- Inter is installed system-wide (`@fontsource/inter`, OFL) and embeds in the PDF.

## 5. Layout recipes, A4 portrait (210 × 297 mm)

**Page frame (all pages unless stated)**
- Margins: left/right 18 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm. Content area 174 × 257 mm.
- Grid: 12 columns, 4 mm gutters (column 10.83 mm). Main split: 1/3 (55.3 mm) + gutter 4 + 2/3 (114.7 mm).
- Folio [inferred, board has none]: footer baseline at 287 mm. Page number 8 pt Bold #2C7B6B on the right. Running title 7.5 pt #5F6368 on the left. No rule.
- Radius tokens (board ratio ≈ 13–17% of the shape's short side, measured on slides 3, 8, 16, 27; tamed slightly for print):
  - `r-panel` 8 mm for shapes with short side ≥ 60 mm
  - `r-card` 4 mm for 20–60 mm
  - `r-chip` 2 mm for ≤ 15 mm
  - `r-photo` 5 mm
  - `pill` = height/2
  - Use only these.

1. **Pill slab.** Rounded rectangle with r = h/2. Heights: 6 mm (tag), 8 mm (button/contact), 24–40 mm (statement pill). Text centred vertically, horizontal padding = h/2 so text never enters the semicircles. Fill #379A86 if the text is ≥14 pt Bold, else #2C7B6B; white pill with #2C7B6B text on teal grounds. [seen 1, 2, 11]
2. **Half-pill edge slab.** Pill with one end cut square and flush to the page edge (bleed 3 mm). Height 100–120 mm on A4, length 150–170 mm, so the straight segment ≥ 1× the radius. Used on cover and closing only. [seen 1]
3. **Rounded panel.** Rectangle r 8 mm (≥ 60 mm short side) or r 4 mm (cards). Padding = max(r, 8 mm). Text panels #2C7B6B; decorative/large-type panels #379A86. Never more than one large panel per page. [seen 3, 8, 16, 17]
4. **Break page.** See R3. One device per report (full colour, panel or photo). Section number in a white circle chip on the panel edge. Frequency: one per chapter, at most 1 per 6 pages. [seen 4, 11, 15, 23]
5. **Section number chip.** Circle Ø 16–18 mm, white with a 12–14 pt Bold #2C7B6B number. Its centre lies exactly on the panel's edge, half on and half off. [seen 4, 11, 15, 23]
6. **Title dot.** See §4 recipe. #379A86 on white, white on teal. It goes on every H1 and never on H2/H3. [seen on all content slides]
7. **Icon chip.** Square, r = 20% of side (12 mm → 2.4 mm). #2C7B6B fill with a white glyph at 58% of side, or white fill with a teal glyph on teal grounds. One icon family only (Lucide/Tabler line icons, 1.5 px stroke, rendered PNG at 600 dpi), or numerals in place of icons. Board size ≈ 6.8% of slide width. [seen 5, 7, 9, 10, 21]
8. **Timeline node.** Circle Ø 14–16 mm #2C7B6B, split year in white 9 pt Bold, connector 1–1.5 pt #379A86. [seen 6]
9. **Square teal field.** Half-page block bleeding three edges, no rounding, used with a straddling rounded photo or a chart. #379A86 (graphics/large type only). [seen 12, 13, 21, 24]
10. **Capsule / D photo crop.** Photo masked to a stadium (r = h/2), or a D bleeding off one edge. Pre-mask in PIL. Max one per page. [seen 2, 5, 7, 10, 17]
11. **Corner blob.** Teal quarter-round or rounded-corner block bleeding a page corner, offset behind a photo or grid by 6–15 mm. [seen 9, 14, 18, 19, 20, 22]
12. **KPI tile.** 40.5 × 36 mm, r 4, #2C7B6B / #3C3D40 alternating, white content. [seen 27, adapted]
13. **Tint callout** [inferred, not on the board]. #E7F3F0, r 4, ink text. This is the quiet panel the board lacks and that a report needs.

## 8. Best for / avoid for

**Best for**
- Report types: company profiles, capability statements, agency or startup proposals, impact reports for NGOs or social enterprises, programme or project updates, onboarding and employee handbooks, event or service brochures, customer-facing annual summaries.
- Industries: services, SaaS/tech, education, community health, sustainability/environment (teal fits), hospitality, design agencies.
- Tone: friendly, optimistic, accessible, modern, "approachable expert".
- Audience: clients, prospects, public, staff, funders who skim.
- Length: 8–30 pages. It suits a high share of visual pages (KPI, timeline, services) and short narrative runs.

**Avoid for**
- Dense analytical, legal, academic or financial-regulatory reports (long text and many tables): the rounded masses fight long reading, and tables cannot be rounded.
- Crisis or bad-news communications: the cheerful teal undermines gravity.
- Luxury or heritage brands. Institutions that need austerity (central banks, courts).
- Documents over about 50 pages: break pages and panels become repetitive.
- Brands whose colour is not teal, unless the accent is swapped and contrast re-checked (the deep/bright pairing rule carries over).

## 9. Do not copy

- **Small white text on bright teal** (slides 3, 4, 8, 11, 16): 3.43:1 fails AA. Use #2C7B6B behind small text.
- **"Rounded everything"**: the board rounds photos, panels, chips, tiles, maps and blobs at different radii. Keep the three radius tokens plus the pill, and leave tables, rules and text columns square. Rounding is the accent, not the texture.
- **Stock-office photos and device mockups** (phone or monitor on slides 21, 22, laptop on desk): generic and dated. Use real project imagery, or nothing.
- **Puzzle-piece infographic** (slide 25) and **map with % bubbles** (slide 26): clichés that encode no data. Use a real chart.
- **Pricing tiles with teal numerals on charcoal** (3.17:1). Reuse the tile shape for KPIs only.
- **Social-media icon circles on the cover**, "Hello guys and welcome", "Our Report Chart Slide", and placeholder titles that describe the slide: report titles must state the finding.
- **Space before "?"** and Title Case subheads ("Who We Are ?"): use sentence case and no space.
- **Q&A subhead trio** ("Who we are / What we do / How we work") on every page: fine once in a profile, filler elsewhere.
- **Two-tone teal/grey decoration** (puzzle, map greys at 1.78:1): grey fills carry no meaning and look washed out in print.
- **Overlapping circular photos** (slide 18): busy, and hard to reproduce in DOCX.

## 10. DOCX implementability

Tested on 2026-10-07 with python-docx and LibreOffice (`soffice --headless --convert-to pdf`), test files in the scratch `docx/` folder. Three methods work in LibreOffice [seen in render]:

- **A. Rendered PNG behind editable text.** Draw the rounded shape with PIL (`ImageDraw.rounded_rectangle`, 4× supersample, 300 dpi, RGBA). Insert it as a `wp:anchor` with `behindDoc="1"`, `wrapNone`, `positionH/V relativeFrom="page"`. Place the text in a borderless one-cell table (exact row height, cell margins = panel padding) at the same coordinates.
  - Pros: text stays fully editable and in the flow, it is the most robust option, and corners are pixel-perfect.
  - Cons: the panel is fixed-size and does not grow with the text. The builder must measure or limit content, and editing in Word can overflow it.
  - python-docx has no anchor API. Convert `wp:inline` → `wp:anchor` in XML (≈25 lines).
  - Not tested: `relativeFrom="paragraph"`, which would let the panel move with reflow [uncertain].
- **B. DrawingML shape.** `wps:wsp` with `prstGeom prst="roundRect"` and text in `wps:txbx/w:txbxContent`. Radius: `adj = r / min(w,h) × 100000`. Verified: adj 8000 on a 174 × 100 mm shape = 8 mm corners, identical to the PNG. Pill: `adj 50000`. Circle: `prst="ellipse"`.
  - Pros: text is editable inside the shape, vector, and the colour can be changed in Word.
  - Cons:
    - Fragile. `noAutofit` boxes do not grow.
    - Small shapes need zero insets: a Ø 16 mm circle with 6 mm insets clipped "02" to "n" in my test.
    - LibreOffice turns shape+text into a shape with an attached frame on round-trip.
    - Word may expect `mc:AlternateContent` with a VML fallback for full compatibility [uncertain]. LibreOffice rendered the bare `wps` fine.
    - Mixing many shapes with table flow can shift anchors.
- **C. Full-page background through the section header.** A PNG anchored `behindDoc` in the header of a dedicated section (unlinked header) covers the whole page (0,0, 210 × 297). Body text flows on top. Verified for a full-colour break page. Robust, and the text stays editable. Best for cover, break and closing pages.
- Native table-cell shading is square only. Word/LibreOffice tables cannot have rounded corners.

| Component | Recommended method | Text editable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title dot | **Native** run (Inter U+2022 at 1.4×, raised 0.28×, exact line spacing) | Yes | Verified. Do not use Plex's bullet (square). |
| Pill tag / label / contact pill | **Shape** (roundRect adj 50000, zero vertical inset) or **image** pill + text run in a 1-cell table | Shape: yes. Image: yes (separate text) | For running tags inside text, simpler alternative: native table cell shaded #2C7B6B (square), accepting square ends. Fragile in LO if many. |
| Half-pill cover / closing slab | **Image** via header anchor (method C) + text in body | Yes | Verified. Bleeds off the page cleanly. |
| Rounded panel holding text (contents, services, break panel) | **Image behind 1-cell table** (method A). Shape B if Word-native editing of the panel matters. | Yes (A and B) | A: fix the height at build time and cap entries. B: verified rendering, fragile on edit. |
| Break page, full colour | **Image** via header (C), or native section page colour (not reliable per page in LO [uncertain]) | Yes | Verified. |
| Section number chip (circle on the panel edge) | **Image** (part of the panel PNG, with the number as text in an anchored table/frame), or **shape** ellipse with 0 insets | Shape: yes | Simplest: bake the circle into the panel PNG and put the number in a positioned 1-cell table. |
| Icon chip | **Image** (chip + glyph rendered together, inline) next to text in a table row | Text yes, glyph no | Robust, inline, flows with text. Numeral chips: inline image of a rounded square + number baked in, or a shape. |
| KPI tile | **Image** tile behind a 4-column table with exact row height (A) | Yes | Or square shaded cells (native) if rounding can be dropped. |
| Timeline (nodes + spine) | **Image** spine + nodes in one PNG behind a 2-column table whose rows match the 30 mm pitch | Yes (dates/text in table) | Dates as table text over the node positions, or baked in (then not editable). |
| Rounded / capsule / D photo crops | **Image**, pre-masked with PIL alpha | n/a | Native and robust. |
| Square teal field (half page) | **Image** via header (C), or shaded table cell (native, square) | Yes | Native shading works because it is square. |
| Corner blob | **Image** anchored behind (A) or via header (C) | n/a | Decorative. |
| Charts | **Image** (matplotlib PNG/SVG at 300 dpi) | No (data in caption/table) | Tint panel can be part of the chart image itself (`FancyBboxPatch` with rounded corners). |
| Tables | **Native**, square corners | Yes | Do not round tables. Put the rounded motif in the pill tag above. |
| Tint callout | **Image** rounded panel behind a 1-cell table (A), or a native shaded cell (square) | Yes | For long or variable callouts, use the native square cell: an overflow bug costs more than square corners. |

Bottom line: build rounded geometry as PNG backgrounds (header-anchored for full pages, page-anchored behind fixed-size tables for panels) and keep all text in native paragraphs and tables. Use DrawingML `roundRect` only for small fixed pills or chips when Word-side editing matters. The style survives LibreOffice, but layouts must be fixed-height: the builder has to cap content per panel.
